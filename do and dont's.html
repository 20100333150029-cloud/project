Do's and Don'ts When We Go to the Mountains

Do's

  • Wear comfortable hiking shoes.
  • Carry enough water and food.
  • Check the weather before going.
  • Stay on the marked hiking trails.
  • Carry a first-aid kit.
  • Keep the mountains clean.
  • Respect plants and animals.

Don'ts

  • Do not throw rubbish on the ground.
  • Do not damage plants or trees.
  • Do not disturb wild animals.
  • Do not go hiking alone in dangerous areas.
  • Do not leave the marked trail.
  • Do not light fires in unsafe places.
  • Do not take unnecessary risks.

Conclusion

Following these do's and don'ts will help us stay safe and protect the beautiful mountain environment.
