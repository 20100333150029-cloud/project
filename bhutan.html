About Bhutan

Bhutan is a beautiful country located in the Eastern Himalayas. It is famous for its mountains, monasteries, forests and traditional architecture.

Bhutan values Gross National Happiness, environmental conservation and the preservation of its rich cultural heritage.

Famous Places to Visit

1. Paro Taktsang

Also called Tiger's Nest, this famous monastery stands on a cliff in Paro.

2. Punakha Dzong

A beautiful historic fortress located between two rivers.

3. Buddha Dordenma

A magnificent Buddha statue overlooking Thimphu Valley.

4. Phobjikha Valley

A peaceful valley famous for its beautiful landscape and black-necked cranes.

Bhutanese Culture

  • Traditional dress: Gho and Kira
  • Colourful festivals called Tshechus
  • Traditional music and dance
  • Buddhist monasteries and dzongs
  • Delicious food such as Ema Datshi

Travel Tips

  • Respect local customs.
  • Keep the environment clean.
  • Wear comfortable shoes.
  • Carry warm clothes.
  • Respect religious places.
