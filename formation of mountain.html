Main Reasons for the Formation of Mountains

Mountains are mainly formed by movements of the Earth's tectonic plates. These movements can push, fold or break the Earth's crust.

1. Movement of Tectonic Plates

When two tectonic plates move towards each other, the Earth's crust can be pushed upwards and form mountains.

2. Volcanic Activity

Some mountains are formed when magma rises from inside the Earth and erupts through a volcano.

3. Folding of Rocks

Strong pressure from moving tectonic plates can fold layers of rock and create mountain ranges.

4. Faulting

Mountains can also form when large blocks of the Earth's crust move along cracks called faults.
